G1R_MegaMod - the five presets of the settings app
==================================================

Above its pages the settings app has a box "Preset" with five sets of
settings, from the game itself to as easy as the settings allow. "Apply
preset" sets everything that makes the game easier or harder, on all pages
at once; nothing is written until you press Save. The box stands on the
preset your settings are at ("- in use"), or on "Your own settings" when
they are at none of the five.

  1 - Base game (hardest)   The game as it is: nothing comes back, nothing
                            regenerates, every number is the game's own.
  2 - Relaxed               A little of everything.
  3 - Easy                  The pace the mod is usually played at.
  4 - Very easy
  5 - Easiest               Every setting at the end of its range that makes
                            the game easier.

A preset does not touch: keys, notes on screen and log switches, the melee
clean-ups, waiting, the map markers, respawn settings of single species,
the technical settings (page "Advanced") and what is not in the app. It
switches the parts it sets on (a module that was switched off in its own
settings is on again).

Below: the name of each setting as it stands in the module's config.lua,
and its value in the five presets.

Respawn and crime (module repopulate)

  Creatures.Enabled             no        yes       yes       yes       yes
  Creatures.NormalChance        35 %      15 %      35 %      60 %      100 %
  Creatures.NormalEveryHours    24        48        24        24        12
  Creatures.EliteChance         15 %      5 %       15 %      30 %      100 %
  Creatures.EliteEveryHours     24        72        24        24        12
  Herbs.Enabled                 no        yes       yes       yes       yes
  Herbs.RegrowHours             24        72        24        12        1
  WorldItems.Enabled            no        yes       yes       yes       yes
  WorldItems.DailyChance        15 %      5 %       15 %      30 %      100 %
  Chests.Enabled                no        yes       yes       yes       yes
  Chests.SettlementDailyChance  30 %      10 %      30 %      50 %      100 %
  Chests.WildDailyChance        10 %      3 %       10 %      25 %      100 %
  Crime: theft                  crime     crime     crime     crime     allowed
  Crime: trespassing            crime     crime     crime     allowed   allowed
  Crime: drawn weapons          crime     crime     allowed   allowed   allowed
  On in every preset: Chests.IncludeLootObjects (loot spots restock with
  the containers) and Crime.ForgetOldCrimes (what was done of the kinds a
  preset allows is forgotten).
  In preset 1 the chances and hours are the mod's usual ones; they count
  again when a part is switched on by hand. In preset 5 creatures come back
  twice a day: the shortest interval the settings allow (1 hour) would only
  crowd the paths again.

Lock picking (module locks)
                                1         2         3         4         5
  UntrainedConnections          game      1         2         2         all
  SkilledConnections            game      2         2         all       all
  MasterConnections             game      game      safe      all       all
  PicksNeverBreak               no        no        no        no        yes
  UntrainedWrongMoves           0         4         6         10        99
  SkilledWrongMoves             0         6         8         15        99
  MasterWrongMoves              0         8         10        20        99
  (game = as the game has it)
  Wrong moves: 0 = the game's own number (2 untrained, 4 skilled, 6
  master). The presets use fixed numbers of connections, which count for
  doors and chests alike; "safe" (as many as the lock stays openable with)
  is only used for the master, where the game's own number is the floor.
  Put back to their defaults by every preset: Enabled.
